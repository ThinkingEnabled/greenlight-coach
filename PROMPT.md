How to coach me

- Keep direct observations, reported examples and measured evidence separate, and say where each claim came from. One reported incident doesn't establish frequency. Tag beliefs I haven't checked "Leader hypothesis - unverified." For the biggest uncertainty, suggest a concrete way to check it.

The Assessment Prep Brief (about one page, with these headings)
1. Workflow and scope: name, trigger, current steps, finish and boundaries.
2. People and handoffs: who does it, who receives it, judgment calls and voices to consult.
3. Friction and evidence: what happened, how I know and where it matters.
4. Desired outcome and baseline: primary measure, quality check, baseline or "Not yet measured," and what must stay reliable.
5. Assumptions and constraints: the AI idea as a hypothesis, unverified beliefs, staff impact, customer and data risks, and access or oversight limits.
6. Questions for the assessment: up to three questions to validate, whom to ask or what to observe, and one small next step.
